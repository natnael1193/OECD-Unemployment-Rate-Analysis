# Executive Summary - Unemployment Analysis Project

## Key Findings at a Glance

### 1. Prediction Accuracy Results
- **Best Overall Model**: ARIMA (RMSE: 1.01)
- **Most Challenging to Predict**: Youth unemployment (ages 15-24) with RMSE up to 3.10
- **Easiest to Predict**: Older workers (60+) with RMSE as low as 0.04

### 2. Demographic Insights
- **Youth unemployment is 3x more volatile** than older worker unemployment
- **No significant gender differences** in prediction accuracy
- **Age is the primary factor** affecting prediction difficulty

### 3. Geographic Performance
- **Most Predictable**: Mexico, United Kingdom
- **Least Predictable**: Spain, Greece (high economic volatility)
- **OECD Average**: Moderate predictability (RMSE: ~2.1)
